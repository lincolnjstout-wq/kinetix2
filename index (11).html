2026 Ford F-150 SuperCrew — Ladder Frame

Loading frame geometry…

The translucent ball previews exactly where the impact will launch from — drag the sliders above to aim anywhere around the frame in a full sphere, then run the test. Wheel contact points hold the truck to the ground (as if parked on its tires) while the rails, crossmembers, and spring hangers flex and can permanently bend or snap under load — individual members visibly spark and scorch at the exact point they fail. On top of that local damage, a hard hit also rocks, dips, and settles the whole vehicle for a moment on its suspension, the way a real parked truck would, before it comes back to rest square on its tires. The body shell is semi-transparent so you can watch the frame underneath, but it's not just a prop: it's mounted to the rails and rides along with whatever the frame (and the whole-vehicle rock) does, and it's solid — the ball can't pass through it — denting and darkening the aluminum skin at the hit point independently of the frame's own steel deformation underneath.
